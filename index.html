Questions

Before you download.

The things people ask first.

Is my health data private?

Yes. swithAI reads steps, active energy, water and sleep from Apple Health or Health Connect and never writes anything back. We do not sell, rent or share your personal data, and you can ask for everything to be deleted at any time. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone, from the App Store, and Android, from Google Play.

Is it really free?

The core app is free and stays free: logging, Health sync, streaks, history and a planner that runs a week ahead. swithAI Plus is a monthly subscription that takes the planner out to 52 weeks and adds generated training plans, the workout recommender and the meal planner with its grocery list. The price is shown in the store before you subscribe.

Does it replace my doctor or dietitian?

No. swithAI provides general wellness, fitness and nutritional information for educational purposes. It is not medical advice, and it is not a substitute for a qualified professional.

Health & safety

swithAI provides general wellness, fitness, and nutritional information for educational purposes only. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition, and it is not a substitute for professional medical, nutritional, or mental health advice.
