swithAI

A wellness app that keeps training, nutrition, activity, and mind & wellness in one place — so the whole picture is easier to see than any single number.

What it does

  • Training Log workouts — exercises, sets, reps, weight, and how hard it felt.
  • Nutrition Search a food database, enter meals manually, and track macros and hydration.
  • Activity Steps, active minutes, cardio sessions, and everyday movement.
  • Mind & Wellness Breathing sessions, mood journaling, and sleep records.
  • Progress Weekly trends and suggestions drawn from what you have actually logged.

Health & safety

swithAI provides general wellness, fitness, and nutritional information for educational purposes only. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition, and it is not a substitute for professional medical, nutritional, or mental health advice.

Talk to a qualified healthcare professional before starting any new exercise or nutrition program, particularly if you have an existing medical condition or are under medical supervision.
